# AI Assistant — Personal Study Space

AI Assistant is an AI-powered personal learning platform designed to help students organize their study materials, understand new concepts, practice through interactive assessments, and track their learning progress.

The platform brings together AI assistance, document management, personalized study notes, flashcards, quizzes, and performance tracking in one workspace. It also helps students understand what they have learned, identify their weak areas, and focus on topics that need improvement.

## Core Features

### 1. Overview (Dashboard)
- Get a complete overview of learning activities and academic performance.
- View average quiz scores to understand overall performance.
- Check recent quiz submissions and their results.
- Access previous quiz attempts through submission history.
- Keep track of recent learning activities from one dashboard.

### 2. Universal Assistant
- Ask questions about any topic and get AI-generated responses.
- Explore ideas, clarify concepts, and get help with problem-solving.
- Use a general-purpose AI chat workspace for everyday learning and assistance.

### 3. Teach Me
- Choose any topic and explain what you have understood in your own words.
- Submit your explanation for AI evaluation.
- Get feedback on the accuracy and completeness of your understanding.
- Identify missing points, incorrect information, and concepts that need more explanation.
- Receive suggestions about what to improve or include in your explanation.

### 4. Documents
- Upload and manage study documents and PDF files.
- Preview uploaded documents directly within the application.
- **Quick Summary:** Get a short overview of a document to understand its main content quickly.
- **Generate Summary:** Generate an AI-powered summary of the document.
- **Chat with Documents:** Ask questions and discuss document-related topics with AI.
- Maintain separate document conversations and revisit previous chats through chat history.
- Reuse uploaded documents across different learning tools.

### 5. Study Notes
- Generate structured study notes from uploaded documents or newly added PDFs.
- Combine multiple PDFs to generate notes from different study materials.
- Customize note generation based on individual learning needs:

  - **Scope:** Entire Document or Specific Topic
  - **Note Format:** Detailed Study Notes, Short Revision Notes, Exam Preparation Notes, or Topic-wise Notes
  - **Difficulty:** Beginner, Intermediate, or Advanced
  - **Language:** English, Bangla, or Bangla-English

- **Readable Formatting:** Organizes content at natural sentence boundaries instead of breaking sentences in the middle.
- **Color-Coded Examples:** Displays examples in separate visual cards with themes compatible with Light and Dark modes.
- **Structured Content:** Organizes definitions, key concepts, formulas, steps, lists, and important takeaways.
- **Note Management:** Search, edit, copy, and download notes in Markdown (`.md`) format.
- **Note History:** Access previously generated notes whenever needed.
- **Create New Note:** Start a fresh note without losing saved notes from history.

### 6. Flashcards
- Generate flashcards from previously uploaded documents or newly added PDFs.
- Choose specific topics or generate cards from study materials.
- Generate between 1 and 20 flashcards in a single request.
- Review important concepts and definitions through interactive flashcards.
- Use active recall for better memorization and revision.

### 7. Quizzes
- Generate quizzes from uploaded documents, PDFs, or specific topics.
- Choose the number of questions from 1 to 20.
- Take timed quizzes with a visible timer to track the time spent.
- Receive immediate feedback after answering each question.
- See whether an answer is correct or incorrect.
- Get explanations for incorrect answers to understand mistakes.
- Review previous quiz attempts and check their results.
- Track quiz performance to understand learning progress.

### 8. Knowledge Gaps
- Analyze quiz performance to understand topic-wise knowledge levels.
- Identify strong topics, weak topics, and areas that need improvement.
- View topic-wise mastery and knowledge gaps.
- Understand which concepts require more practice and revision.
- Use performance insights to focus on areas that need attention.

### 9. Improvement
- Track daily learning activity and study consistency.
- Monitor the number of active days and overall activity.
- View activity patterns across different dates.
- Identify days with higher study activity and days with little or no activity.
- Understand learning consistency over time.

### 10. Profile & Theme Support
- Manage personal profile information and account settings.
- Register and log in using Firebase Authentication.
- Switch between Light and Dark themes.
- Enjoy a clean and comfortable interface across different study environments.

## Project Vision

The idea behind AI Assistant is to make learning more interactive and personalized.

Instead of simply reading study materials and memorizing information, students can actively engage with what they learn. They can explain concepts in their own words, receive feedback, test their understanding, identify knowledge gaps, and monitor their improvement over time.

The platform aims to bring the entire learning process into one personal study space, helping students become more independent, consistent, and confident learners.
